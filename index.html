Popular Destinations

manali

Manali, known as the "Valley of the Gods," is a scenic high-altitude hill station in India's Himachal Pradesh state, situated in the Kullu Valley.

Kashmir

Kashmir, often called "Paradise on Earth," is world-renowned for its breathtaking natural beauty, including the Himalayas, Dal Lake, and tulip gardens, alongside rich cultural heritage.

shimla

Shimla, known as the "Queen of Hills," is the capital of Himachal Pradesh, India, located in the northwest Himalayas at an elevation of over 2,000m.
